4) Daily automation cadence

  1. Morning, 20 minutes: open saved searches, shortlist roles posted in the last 24 hours, and add them to the tracker.
  2. Midday, 30 minutes: tailor CV headline and top 6 bullets to the job keywords before applying.
  3. Evening, 20 minutes: send 5 targeted LinkedIn messages to recruiters, FM operations leaders, or site managers.

Weekly KPI targets

  • 25 quality applications or direct career-page submissions.
  • 25 recruiter/hiring-manager messages.
  • 10 referral conversations with UAE FM professionals.
  • 2 CV variants: hard FM/MEP and soft services/operations.

Avoid fully automated mass applying; UAE employers still respond best to relevant CVs and referrals.
