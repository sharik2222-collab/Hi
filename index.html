Quick Start (15-Minute Sequence)

  1. Rewrite headline with role + niche + quantified result.
  2. Update About section with strategy + proof metrics.
  3. Rewrite top 2 roles with outcome-led bullets.
  4. Add missing ATS keywords from 3 target job descriptions.
  5. Score profile and fix the lowest-scoring category first.

1) Positioning Statement

Use one core message across headline, About, and experience bullets:

I help [target audience] achieve [business outcome] through [core capabilities].

2) Headline Formula

[Role] | [Expertise] | [Business outcomes] | [Industry context]
  • Include your target role keyword.
  • Add 2–4 high-intent skills.
  • Add at least one measurable impact metric.

3) About Template

I am a [role] with [X]+ years in [domain], focused on [business outcomes].
I led initiatives delivering [metric], [metric], [metric].
Known for strategic thinking + disciplined execution.
Open to: [roles], [industries], [locations/work model].

4) Experience Bullet Pattern

Action + Scope + Business Result (+ metric) + Strategic context

Example: Led pricing redesign across 3 lines, increased gross margin by 8.2 pts.

5) ATS Keyword Strategy

  • Collect 20–30 keywords from target job descriptions.
  • Distribute across headline, About, experience, and skills.
  • Use exact recurring phrases naturally.

6) Weekly Opportunity Plan

  • 1 post/week: insight, proof, POV, or leadership note.
  • Comment on 5–10 relevant leaders weekly.
  • Run a 30-minute optimization sprint each week.

Target score: 45/50+

Red Flags That Reduce Interviews

  • Generic headline with no specialization or impact.
  • About section without hard metrics or business outcomes.
  • Experience bullets listing responsibilities only.
  • No clear target role (trying to target everyone).
  • Weak ATS keyword overlap with target jobs.
