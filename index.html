Quick Start (30 Minutes)

  1. Rewrite your headline with role + niche + quantified impact.
  2. Rewrite About section using the 5-part structure.
  3. Upgrade top 2 roles with outcome-driven bullets.
  4. Add missing keywords from 3 target job descriptions.
  5. Score profile and fix the lowest category first.

CEO / Founder Track

Headline: CEO | [Market] | Scaled [Revenue/Users] | [Specialty]

ATS keywords: CEO, Founder, P&L, Fundraising, GTM, Board Reporting.

  • Scaled revenue from $4.2M to $11.8M in 24 months.
  • Improved gross margin by 9.1 points via pricing redesign.

VP / Director Track

Headline: VP/Director of [Function] | [Domain] | Delivered [Outcome]

ATS keywords: Transformation, Forecasting, KPI, Stakeholder Management, Change Leadership.

  • Reduced process cycle time by 37% across 5 workflows.
  • Improved planning forecast accuracy from 68% to 91%.

Senior IC Track

Headline: Senior [Role] | [Tools] | Improved [Metric] in [Context]

ATS keywords: Optimization, Analytics, SQL, Automation, Experimentation.

  • Improved activation from 42% to 64% through onboarding redesign.
  • Automated reporting, saving 18 hours per month.

Positioning Statement

I help [target audience] achieve [business outcome] through [capabilities].

Experience Bullet Pattern

Action + Scope + Business Result (+ metric) + Strategic Context

About (5-Part Structure)

  1. Who you are
  2. What you solve
  3. Proof metrics
  4. Leadership style
  5. Call to action

Before vs After

Headline before: Operations Manager
After: Operations Leader | Process Automation | Reduced cycle time 38% across 12 regions

About before: Experienced professional in operations.
After: I lead programs improving speed, quality, and margin — recently reducing cycle time 31% and increasing on-time delivery to 97.8%.

Bullet before: Responsible for onboarding.
After: Redesigned onboarding across product + support, increasing activation from 44% to 66% and reducing time-to-value by 9 days.
