# Hi

## Choose Your Track

Use the track that matches your target role and copy only that section into your LinkedIn profile or resume draft.

### 1) CEO/Founder Track

**Sample headline template**
- `CEO & Founder | [Industry/Category] | Built [Company/Product] to [Key Outcome] | Raising/Scaling [Stage]`

**Sample About opening line**
- `I build and scale companies that solve [specific market problem], turning early vision into measurable growth.`

**Top 10 must-have ATS keywords**
- P&L Ownership
- Fundraising
- Go-to-Market Strategy
- Business Development
- Strategic Partnerships
- Revenue Growth
- Market Expansion
- Product Strategy
- Board Communication
- Organizational Leadership

**3 example achievement bullets**
- Secured **$8M Series A** from tier-1 investors and scaled team from **12 to 55** in 18 months.
- Launched multi-channel GTM strategy that increased ARR from **$1.2M to $6.7M** year over year.
- Expanded into **3 new markets**, driving **42%** of total revenue from new geographies.
